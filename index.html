Spam SMS Blocker

Turkish App Store name: Spam SMS Engelleyici. Developer: Mustafa Demir.

Spam SMS Blocker is an iOS app that filters SMS and MMS messages from unknown senders into your Junk folder. It uses Apple's Message Filter extension, and all filtering happens on your device. Your messages are never sent anywhere.
