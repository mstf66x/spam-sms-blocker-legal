Privacy Policy

Spam SMS Blocker (iOS). Developer: Mustafa Demir. Effective date: 13 November 2026.

Summary

Spam SMS Blocker filters your messages entirely on your device. Your message text, senders and custom rules never leave your device. The app collects only anonymous usage analytics to help improve the app.

How message filtering works

The app uses Apple's Message Filter extension (the IdentityLookup framework). For SMS and MMS messages from numbers that are not in your contacts, iOS gives the extension the sender and the message text so it can decide whether the message should be filtered.

  • Filtering is fully on-device.
  • The extension has no network access (Apple enforces this) and cannot store anything.
  • Message text, senders and your custom rules are never transmitted, sold or shared.
  • Your rules and settings are stored locally in the app's App Group container on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • The in-app "Test" feature classifies the text you paste on your device only.

Anonymous analytics

The app uses PostHog (PostHog Inc., cloud-hosted, US region) for anonymous product analytics.

What is collected:

  • App interaction events: screens viewed, onboarding steps, filters turned on or off, the number and type of custom rules (never their content), and the result category of the in-app test (never the text).
  • Device and app information: iOS version, device model, app version, and language/locale.
  • A random anonymous identifier generated when the app is installed.

IP addresses are not stored (IP anonymization is enabled).

What is not collected: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, message content, senders, location, or advertising identifier.

There is no cross-app tracking, no advertising and no sale of data. Analytics data is used only to improve the app. It is retained according to PostHog's retention settings, and you can request its deletion at any time by emailing hello@conjugaizen.com.

Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may request access to, deletion of, or object to the processing of data related to you. To exercise these rights, contact hello@conjugaizen.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
